# Overview

<UsageAdvice permitted>
  Use this component whenever you need users to enter a phone number.

As with all personal information, consider whether you need to collect it at all. Clearly explain why you're asking for the phone number and what will be done to ensure the user's privacy.

</UsageAdvice>

<UsageAdvice permitted={false}>
  Do not use this component to ask for any information other than a phone
  number.
</UsageAdvice>

## Principles

Tell users why you need their phone number, why and when you might contact them.

<CodePreview
  scriptPath="/scripts/components/forms-phone-input.js"
  minHeight="400px"
  code={PhoneInputComponent({
    title: 'Phone number',
    hint: 'Used only to contact you if there are any problems with your application',
  })}
/>
